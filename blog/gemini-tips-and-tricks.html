10 Gemini Tips and Tricks You Didn't Know (2026)

Hidden features and power-user techniques to get 10x more value from Google Gemini.

Google Gemini is packed with features that most users never discover. After months of daily use, I've compiled the best tips and tricks that have transformed how I use this AI assistant.

Whether you're new to Gemini or a regular user, these hidden gems will help you work faster and get better results.

1 Use Voice Mode for Hands-Free Conversations

Click the microphone icon to talk to Gemini instead of typing. This isn't just speech-to-text - it's a natural conversation mode where Gemini responds with voice too.

Best for: Brainstorming, cooking (recipe help), driving, or when you're tired of typing.

2 Upload Images for Instant Analysis

Drag and drop any image into Gemini for instant analysis. It can read text from photos, explain diagrams, identify objects, solve math problems from pictures, and even critique design work.

Try this: Upload a photo of a restaurant menu in another language - Gemini will translate and explain each dish.

3 Connect to Google Workspace

Enable the Google Workspace extension in Gemini settings. Once connected, you can ask Gemini to search your Gmail, summarize Google Docs, find files in Drive, and check your Calendar - all from the chat.

"Find the email from last week about the project deadline and summarize it"

4 Use @ Mentions for Extensions

Type @ to access Gemini's extensions directly in your prompt. You can mention @YouTube to search videos, @Maps for location info, or @Flights for travel searches.

"@YouTube find tutorials on Python decorators for beginners"

5 Ask for Multiple Options

Don't settle for the first response. Ask Gemini to give you 3-5 different options, approaches, or variations. This works great for naming ideas, email drafts, coding solutions, and creative writing.

"Give me 5 different ways to start an email declining a meeting politely"

8 Use Gemini for Google Sheets Formulas

Struggling with a spreadsheet formula? Describe what you want in plain English, and Gemini will write the exact formula for you - including complex nested functions.

"Write a Google Sheets formula that counts cells in column A where column B is 'Complete' and column C is greater than 100"

Copy the formula directly into your sheet. No more Googling syntax!

9 Set a Custom Persona

Start your conversation by telling Gemini who it should be. This dramatically improves response quality for specialized tasks.

"You are a senior software engineer at Google with 15 years of experience. I'm going to ask you about system design."

Other useful personas: Writing coach, nutritionist, travel agent, legal advisor, UX designer

Bonus: Prompt Techniques

These prompting strategies work with any AI, but they're especially effective with Gemini:

  • "Think step by step" - Forces more thorough, accurate reasoning
  • "What are the downsides?" - Gets balanced perspective instead of just positives
  • "Explain like I'm 5" - Simplifies complex topics
  • "Be concise" - Prevents overly long responses
  • "Give me a table" - Structures information for easy scanning
