Website Analytics

If you allow analytics, browserlab.io uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which sources bring visitors to the website, which pages they visit, and which extension store links they select. These store-link clicks are not installation counts. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives technical information about your visit, such as browser and device information. We do not enable advertising signals or advertising personalization.

We do not send pasted transcripts, text entered into website tools, Gemini conversations, contact details, arbitrary URL queries, or URL fragments to Google Analytics. The transcript cleaner processes your text in your browser. Website analytics is separate from the extensions and the Chrome Web Store's own analytics.

You can allow or decline analytics when prompted, and change your choice using the Analytics preferences button at the bottom of a page. Declining analytics does not prevent you from using the website. Google is not loaded for website analytics before you allow it. Learn more about Google's privacy policy.

Browser Storage and Cookies

We save your analytics preference in your browser so you do not have to choose on every page. After you allow analytics, we also keep a limited landing-page and traffic-source record in browser session storage and let Google Analytics use cookies for website measurement. If you turn analytics off, we stop future website analytics, remove that session record, and clear this website property's analytics cookies. Your preference remains saved. We do not use these analytics cookies for advertising.
