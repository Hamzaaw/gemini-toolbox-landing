How to Bulk Delete Gemini Chats (Free)

The fastest free method to delete multiple Google Gemini conversations at once. Clean up 100+ chats in 30 seconds.

TL;DR

The fastest way to bulk delete Gemini chats: Install Bulk Delete for Gemini (free Chrome extension), open Gemini, select the chats you want to remove, click delete. Done in under 30 seconds.

If you've been using Google Gemini for a while, you probably have dozens (or hundreds) of old conversations cluttering your sidebar. Test chats, quick questions, experiments - they pile up fast.

The problem? Gemini doesn't have a built-in way to delete multiple chats at once. You have to click each conversation, find the delete option, and confirm - one by one. For 100 chats, that's 15+ minutes of tedious clicking.

The solution: Use Bulk Delete for Gemini, a free Chrome extension that adds multi-select deletion to Gemini. Here's how it works.

Why Delete Old Gemini Chats?

There are several good reasons to clean up your Gemini chat history:

  • Performance: Too many chats can slow down Gemini's interface, especially when loading the sidebar
  • Organization: A cleaner chat list makes it easier to find important conversations
  • Privacy: Remove sensitive or personal conversations you no longer need
  • Fresh start: Sometimes you just want to start clean without old clutter

Step-by-Step: Bulk Delete Gemini Chats

Step 1: Install Bulk Delete for Gemini

Go to the Chrome Web Store and click "Add to Chrome". The extension installs in about 30 seconds.

Note: The extension only requests access to gemini.google.com - it can't see any other websites.

Step 2: Open Gemini and Enable Bulk Delete Mode

Navigate to gemini.google.com. You'll see new controls added by the extension. Click the bulk delete toggle to enable selection mode.

Checkboxes will appear next to each conversation in your sidebar.

Step 3: Select Chats to Delete

Click the checkbox next to each chat you want to remove. You have two options:

  • Select individually: Click specific chats you want to delete
  • Select all: Click the "Select All" button to check every conversation at once

Step 4: Delete Selected Chats

Click the "Delete Selected" button. You'll see a confirmation prompt showing how many chats will be deleted. Confirm, and they're gone instantly.

Tip: Export important chats to PDF first using SaveChat for Gemini before deleting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover deleted Gemini chats?

No, once you delete chats from Gemini, they're permanently removed. That's why we recommend exporting important conversations to PDF before deleting. The extension shows a confirmation prompt to prevent accidental deletions.

Is this safe to use?

Yes. The extension only deletes the chats you explicitly select. It processes everything locally in your browser and doesn't send any data to external servers. Your conversations stay private.

Will this delete chats from all my Google accounts?

No, it only affects the Google account you're currently logged into on Gemini. If you have multiple accounts, you'd need to switch accounts and repeat the process.

Does it work with Gemini Advanced?

Yes, Bulk Delete for Gemini works with all Gemini versions including free, Advanced, and Workspace accounts.

How many chats can I delete at once?

There's no limit. The extension can handle hundreds of chats at once without browser crashes or timeouts.
